I recently started using **Kimi**, the AI assistant built on the K3 flagship model, together with **Kimi Code**, its programming agent that works in the terminal.

This website is the first fruit of that: I made the entire homepage — from choosing the al-folio template, writing the configuration and biography, to adding my publications and deploying it to GitHub Pages — in conversation with Kimi Code. The model read my old homepage and my faculty page, filled in my information, cropped my portrait, and pushed every change; I only reviewed and directed.

I plan to keep writing here about algebraic geometry, and probably more about my experience using AI tools in research and daily work.
